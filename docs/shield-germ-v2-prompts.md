## Prompt final

Create a replacement pixel-art animation sprite sheet for this game's shield germ mini-boss. Reference image: current character identity, a green germ holding a large organic slime shield on its LEFT side facing LEFT. Improve it significantly: darker forest/emerald green body, sturdy distinct silhouette, large curved slime shield with bright mint edge, rounded germ protrusions and slime drips, small red eyes, mischievous determined face, appropriate for 9-year-old children. No black armor, no weapons other than the organic shield. Crisp coarse SNES pixel art, about 80 pixels of detail per character, limited palette, dark outline. True transparent background, NO colored rectangles, no ground, shadows, glow, text, borders or labels. Production asset, exactly 4 columns x 4 rows of 16 equal square cells on a square canvas, with comfortable transparent margins. Same character size and scale across frames, feet on same baseline except fall poses, character center consistent, no overlap. ALL poses facing LEFT. Row 1: four subtle idle breathing frames, legs planted shield upright, slight slime dripping. Row 2: 1 crouched windup holding shield in front, 2 charging left with shield out leading and back leg extended, 3 alternate running charge stride, 4 wall-impact recoil with shield tilted and startled face. Row 3: four very readable short-range shield BASH frames: 1 pull shield back toward torso with bent knees, 2 step and thrust shield far LEFT with extended arm, 3 shield fully extended left with a small bright cream crescent motion swipe confined to that cell, 4 return shield toward body and recover. Row 4: four DEFEAT frames: 1 lose balance with closed eyes, 2 knees collapse and body starts falling left, 3 body falls flat onto its side with shield sliding beside it, 4 resting completely prone stretched horizontally on the floor, eyes closed and mouth slack, shield laid on floor beside body. Keep entire prone body inside its cell; full genuine drawn collapse and prone poses, not a rotated standing sprite. High-quality readable game animation sheet, no duplicated static poses.
