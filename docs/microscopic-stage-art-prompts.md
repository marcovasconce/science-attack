# Arte da fase microscópica

Assets criados com a ferramenta imagegen integrada. Referência visual fornecida pelo usuário: codex-clipboard-8cc826a7-489f-4ef2-9c05-3c9fcccda977.png. As imagens são usadas por AtlasTexture do Godot, sem alterar os originais gerados.

## background

Create a seamless horizontally tileable pixel-art background plate for a side-scrolling beat-em-up taking place at microscopic scale on the inside surface of a machine. Use the supplied image ONLY as reference for the teal/blue-gray palette, chunky pixel outlines, recessed panel shapes and moody lighting. Remove all characters and all human-scale furniture, shelves, cabinets, counters, computers, monitors, servers, bottles, logos and signs. This is an EMPTY microscopic metallic landscape: immense simple teal panels, seams, very subtle fine scratches and small specks suggesting enlarged material texture. Composition for a 640x360 game viewport: straight-on vertical back wall occupies the upper 52% (0-187px logical), broad walkable metal floor occupies the bottom 48% (187-360px logical), viewed from a shallow three-quarter beat-em-up angle. Flat horizontal wall-floor boundary. The floor must be clear, muted blue-gray steel, broad restrained panel seams, NOT a checkerboard, no objects and no holes. Enough uncluttered floor for two characters to walk in depth. Wall details stay subdued behind HUD. Lighting from top, dark navy recesses, muted cyan panel rims. No glowing sci-fi neon, no perspective vanishing point, no central focal prop. Match left and right border colors and floor/wall heights so duplicate images join horizontally. Pure crisp chunky 16-bit pixel-art, limited palette, same visual family as the provided reference. Output landscape 1536x864, full bleed with no margins, black bars, text, watermark or UI.

## bolts

Create a transparent pixel-art pickup atlas for a beat-em-up. Exactly SIX centered sprites in a uniform 3-column by 2-row grid of square cells. All six sprites are the SAME recognizable chunky short steel machine screw/bolt, shown at slightly different orientations: diagonal down-right, horizontal right, diagonal up-right; diagonal down-left, horizontal left, upright. Every bolt has a broad HEXAGON HEAD, a bright top face with small recessed slot, a thick threaded shaft and blunt tip. No nuts, washers, gears, plates, circuits or other objects. All are silver steel with blue-gray shadow, dark navy outline and crisp white highlights. Very clear coarse pixel clusters designed to read at only 16-20 pixels in game, not photorealistic and not excessively detailed. Keep consistent size and material across cells, each bolt occupies around 55% of a square cell, generous transparent padding, no clipping. Actual transparent RGBA background, no ground shadow, glow, text, numbers, border, grid, watermark or logo.

## bridge

Create one transparent sprite of a modular steel bridge floor for a pixel-art beat-em-up. The bridge is a broad rectangular platform seen at the same shallow three-quarter angle as a side-scroller's walkable floor, running LEFT TO RIGHT across a gap. The art is intended to be divided VERTICALLY into THREE equal adjacent construction sections; each third must fit seamlessly against its neighbor, no gaps between sections. Front edge horizontal and back edge horizontal, no diagonal perspective vanishing, no tall handrails or vertical supports obscuring characters. A continuous industrial steel deck of horizontal ribbed gray-blue plates, bolted metal seams, reinforced dark side beams, a few small muted yellow corner hazard marks. Solid opaque walkable deck, not open grating. Clearly metal, match a teal/navy industrial pixel-art environment. Crisp chunky pixels, limited palette, readable at a final width of 192 and height of 150 game pixels. Output an isolated rectangle filling the image edge-to-edge horizontally and vertically except a very narrow transparent bevel margin. Aspect ratio around 4:3. All three sections identical height and able to appear one at a time left to right. Actual transparent RGBA background outside the deck only, no hole/background, no characters, no machines, no lettering, no labels, no watermark, no diagram grid.

Arquivos: `assets/environment/microscopic_panels.png`, `assets/pickups/construction_bolts.png`, `assets/environment/metal_bridge.png`. Ponte recortada nativamente na região (0, 346, 1448, 468), dividida em três partes. Fundo separado em parede e piso para alinhar a área de movimento. Parafusos: atlas 3×2.
