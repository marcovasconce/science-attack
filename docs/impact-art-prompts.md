# Impactos de combate

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen` (skill `imagegen`), usando as imagens enviadas como referências visuais. A referência de fogo foi usada para criar uma animação original, sem recortar o material de banco de imagens.

Arquivos do projeto: `assets/effects/crate_impact.png` e `assets/effects/enemy_impact.png`. Cada atlas tem seis quadros, organizados em três colunas e duas linhas. A cena `scenes/effects/impact_burst.tscn` faz a leitura em ordem, congela durante a pausa e remove a instância ao terminar.

## Prompt da caixa

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2D beat-em-up impact animation sprite sheet. Input image is the reference for the cyan-white four-point impact star only; discard the game background and characters. Derive a polished original animation of that same visual idea for a pixel-art Godot game. Output exactly SIX animation frames in a uniform 3-column by 2-row grid, read left to right then down. All six cells equal square size, every effect centered at the exact cell center, generous transparent padding, no effect overlapping cells. Sequence: 1 tiny white-cyan ignition star, 2 sharp bright four-point cyan-white impact star with small hollow center, 3 peak star with four long curved tapered points and luminous cyan ring, 4 expanded thinner ring with retracting rays, 5 broken cyan ring and few sparks, 6 faint small dispersing cyan sparks. Sharp pixel-art silhouettes, limited white/ice blue/cyan palette, dark blue edge accents, readable at 64x64 pixels per frame. The largest effect is about 70% of a cell width. Strong hand-crafted arcade VFX, no soft photographic bloom. Actual transparent RGBA background, including hollow centers. No grid lines, text, captions, numbers, border, logos, people, environment or watermark. Preserve one stable contact point throughout, no camera movement.

## Prompt do inimigo

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2D beat-em-up hit impact animation sprite sheet. Input image is only a visual reference for the fiery yellow-orange ring hit effects, not a sheet to copy or clean. Create an original cohesive SIX-frame animation inspired by its star flash and flame ring, with exactly 3 columns and 2 rows of equal square cells. Chronological reading left to right, first row then second row. Each effect precisely centered in its cell with consistent position and generous fully transparent padding. Frame 1 tiny hot white-yellow spark, frame 2 sharp compact bright yellow-white starburst with orange tips, frame 3 bright yellow core opening into an orange flame ring, frame 4 larger uneven circular golden-orange ring with transparent center and curling flame tips, frame 5 broken ring of orange embers, frame 6 few fading orange sparks. Largest frame occupies about 70% of cell width. Pixel-art arcade game VFX with crisp hand-crafted edges and limited white, lemon yellow, gold, orange and red palette, readable at 64x64. Actual transparent RGBA background also inside the rings. No grid, no captions or numbers, no text, no watermark or logo, no characters or environment. Keep all six frames equally spaced, no mixing variants, no clipping.
