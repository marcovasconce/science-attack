## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent pixel-art NPC robot sprite for a 640x360 16-bit side-scrolling beat-em-up. Reference image role: shape/material/color reference only. Create a friendly equipment-supply spider robot based closely on its vertical green energy reservoir, two chunky silver-blue side housings, light-cyan small rectangular indicators and heavy dark outlines. IMPORTANT: EXACTLY FOUR mechanical spider legs, two on each side, all four feet clearly visible and countable. Symmetrical left/right silhouette, two rear legs angled up/out then down, two front legs angled out/down, four distinct bent jointed supports ending in green and navy pointed feet. NO extra arms, pincers, appendages, fifth or sixth leg. View slightly from above/front as in a side-scrolling beat-em-up, body facing viewer. Square robust body over the four legs, central green reservoir and readable silver-blue side blocks, tiny steel rivets, simple equipment access hatch on front. Crisp coarse pixel clusters designed to remain readable as a 100x104 pixel sprite in game; sharp edges, restrained palette, not realistic, no antialiasing. Entire sprite centered with generous transparent padding; no backdrop, no floor, no cast shadow, no glow outside silhouette, no text, no grid, no labels, no weapons, no people. Preserve actual transparent RGBA background.
