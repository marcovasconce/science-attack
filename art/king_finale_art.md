Criada em 19/11/2026 com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen. Imagens com fundo transparente, integradas na versão science-attack-main-(4).

## Prompt: rei
Create a production pixel-art spritesheet for the EXISTING cartoon slime BACTERIA KING shown in reference image 1. Preserve this exact character identity: lime/olive green gooey round germ body, multiple little lobes, big red grinning mouth with ivory teeth, red eyes, gold crown and red jewels, strong slime arms. Transparent background. Output ONE SQUARE 1024x1024 spritesheet, strictly FOUR columns and FOUR rows, sixteen equal 256x256 cells with transparent gutters, absolutely NO grid lines, text, numbers, labels or scenery. True crisp low-resolution 16-bit arcade pixel-art, hard square pixel clusters, no antialiasing, no blurred edges. Each pose rendered as if native approximately 80x80 pixels and enlarged uniformly; match original style, NOT a different monster. All cells align to same foot baseline 220px and center x=128, whole body and crown contained with generous padding. Frames left to right top to bottom: 0 idle squat grin arms low; 1 idle breathing arms low; 2 clearly TWO arms lifting above his head preparing a powerful ground pound; 3 BOTH fists raised fully above crown ready to slam; 4 both arms plunging down together; 5 crouched with BOTH fists hitting ground simultaneously, no external effect; 6 lifting back up from two-fist slam; 7 cheeks puffed both hands near mouth SPITTING a slime projectile, no projectile; 8 mouth very wide INHALING with both arms open; 9 both arms raised sideways commanding geysers; 10 angry roaring arms spread; 11 flinching from hit; 12 losing balance and falling sideways; 13 lying fully horizontal on the ground crown tipped, eyes dazed arms limp; 14 same defeated horizontal prone pose settled; 15 same prone pose eyes closed but comic non-gory. It is essential frames 2-6 use BOTH arms, and frames13-15 are visibly lying down, not squashed standing. Reference is identity/style only; redraw actual new poses. Clean transparent alpha, no cast shadows outside sprite.

## Prompt: onda
Production game effect sprite sheet, transparent alpha background, square 1024x1024 canvas, FOUR columns FOUR rows equal 256px cells (16 frames). Draw one highly visible bright toxic lime and mint green ground-pound shockwave, 16-bit pixel art, chunky sharply stepped square pixels, dark emerald outline, lemon-yellow bright center, cyan-white edges. Side-view beat-em-up, LOW wide curved splash rising into pointed liquid spikes, inspired by a low ground impact splash, not an explosion ball or a tall geyser. Sequence left to right top to bottom: 0 small ground impact star, 1 small spiky splash, 2 growing two outward crests, 3 large bright low wave, 4 maximum tall central spikes with waves spreading to both sides, 5 crests farther outward, 6 split outward wave, 7 wide low double crests, 8 shrinking with scattered droplets, 9 smaller droplets, 10 separated droplets, 11 sparse droplets, 12 fading small beads, 13 few tiny beads, 14 two tiny beads, 15 one tiny bead. Each frame entirely inside its cell, consistent ground baseline at y220, centered x128, generous transparent gutters; animated liquid material with solid readable silhouette. No brown, no background, no characters, no scenery, NO typography/grid/cell numbers, no blurred gradients, no white matte. The effect must remain clearly visible on dark olive sewer flooring.

## Prompt: toca aqui
Create a game animation spritesheet on TRANSPARENT background, using the two tiny game character references as STRICT identity and pixel style references. Ref1 is MAX: tiny white/very pale blue hooded scientist monster with one large dark navy oval face area, one large white pixel eye, white lab-coat-like body and little orange tie. Ref2 is AMANDA: tiny bright pink/magenta monster with two little pointed ears, white/pale belly and one large white eye in a dark navy face patch. Do not turn them into humans or redesign them. No weapons. A paired high-five victory animation with Max on LEFT looking RIGHT and Amanda on RIGHT looking LEFT, facing one another. One square sheet, FOUR columns and TWO rows (eight equal rectangular cells), consistent pair size and ground alignment per cell, wide transparent gutters. Frame0 standing side by side facing each other, frame1 both bend their near arms up, frame2 both reach one hand overhead toward the center between them, frame3 hands touch directly above center, frame4 hands touching in a celebratory pose, frame5 hands separate and bodies little hop, frame6 happy landing arms lowering, frame7 settled celebratory pose. Maintain visible space between their bodies, touch hands only, never overlapping faces. Both characters full body and feet visible in every cell, matching the exact chunky simple 32x32 original character sprites (approximately 30px tall each, enlarged integer pixels, pair approximately 68px wide), dark outline hard square pixel clusters, no detailed painterly shading, no antialiasing. No text, grid, background, labels or extra characters. Do NOT draw the sparkle: it will be animated separately in game.

## Sequência final
Arremesso do antídoto; bolhas preenchem a tela; a tela permanece completamente coberta durante um segundo; revelação da paleta azulada; Max e Amanda se aproximam, tocam as mãos e recebem o brilho; vitória.

As poses do rei e a onda são sprites novos. O cenário reutiliza as texturas da versão atual com uma paleta de limpeza aplicada somente ao ambiente. Os personagens mantêm suas cores.
